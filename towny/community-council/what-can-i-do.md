# ⁉️ What Can I Do?

## Permissions & Limitations

### What Community Council CAN Do

* Organize and host events
* Collaborate with players and towns
* Help decorate/build event spaces
* Suggest server activities
* Represent community interests
* Assist in promoting server engagement

***

### What Community Council CANNOT Do

* Moderate punishments
* Ban or mute players
* Abuse staff permissions
* Promise rewards without approval
* Override server rules
* Speak officially on behalf of administration unless instructed

**The Community Council is a community role, not a disciplinary authority.**
